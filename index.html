Variable Data Printing & Mail Merge Guide
How Leading Zeros Are Preserved

When opened normally, spreadsheet programs like Excel often coerce 0042 into the integer 42. Our generator encapsulates every number inside text quotes (e.g. "0042") and includes a UTF-8 BOM. When imported into Canva, InDesign, or Word, it is recognized as a fixed-length string, preserving every zero on your printed tickets.

C
Canva Bulk Create (Variable Printing)
  1. In Canva, design your raffle ticket with text placeholders for your ticket number and winning numbers.
  2. Click on the Apps tab on the left sidebar and search for Bulk Create.
  3. Select Upload CSV and pick the file downloaded from this generator.
  4. Right-click each text box on your canvas, choose Connect data, and link to Ticket_Number, Winning_Num_1, etc.
  5. Click Continue and then Generate Pages. Canva will create all tickets in batch!
W
Microsoft Word Mail Merge
  1. Open your ticket template in Word (or use a multi-up perforated label sheet).
  2. Go to the Mailings tab > Select Recipients > Use an Existing List...
  3. Select your downloaded CSV.
  4. Insert Merge Fields: «Ticket_Number» and each «Winning_Num_1».
  5. Click Finish & Merge to print or export as PDF.
Id
Adobe InDesign Data Merge
  1. Open Window > Utilities > Data Merge.
  2. From the panel menu, choose Select Data Source and pick the CSV file.
  3. Drag the fields into your master ticket frame.
  4. Choose Create Merged Document to generate single or multi-record sheets with crop marks.
